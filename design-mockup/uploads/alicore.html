ABOUT ALICORE

Building the Elements Behind Exceptional Architecture

Alicore manufactures GFRC and FRP architectural products, engineered with a focus on design flexibility, dimensional precision and material durability. Every product is developed to translate an architectural intent into a component that can be consistently produced.

We work alongside architects, developers, builders, contractors and interior designers — supporting projects from early concept discussions through to manufactured, site-ready elements.
