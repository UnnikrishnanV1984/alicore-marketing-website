BRAND

Company Name: ALICORE

Suggested tagline:
Architectural Products. Engineered to Elevate.

Alternative tagline:
Crafting Architecture. Building Possibilities.

Brand positioning:
ALICORE manufactures high-quality architectural products for architects, builders, developers, contractors and designers. The company specializes in customized architectural elements using GFRC and FRP, combining modern manufacturing technology, engineering and aesthetic design.

The website should communicate:

* Premium quality
* Architectural excellence
* Engineering capability
* Custom manufacturing
* Reliability
* Large-project capability
* Modern manufacturing
* Professionalism
* Innovation

Do NOT make the website look like a small local construction company.

Make it look like an established international architectural-products manufacturer.

DESIGN STYLE

Use a sophisticated architectural aesthetic.

Primary colours:

* Charcoal / near-black
* Warm white
* Light concrete grey
* Subtle metallic/gold accent

Use lots of white space.

Typography:

* Modern premium sans-serif
* Strong bold headings
* Clean readable body text

Visual style:

* Large architectural photographs
* GFRC and FRP close-up textures
* Modern buildings
* Facades
* Decorative jalis
* Architectural panels
* Manufacturing/process photographs
* Minimal animations
* Smooth scrolling
* Premium transitions

* Cheap stock-photo appearance
* Excessive gradients
* Cartoon graphics
* Too many colours
* Crowded layouts
* Generic construction-company templates

FINAL DESIGN DIRECTION

The final website should feel similar to a premium architectural materials brand.

Think:

Architecture + Engineering + Manufacturing + Luxury

The website should immediately make an architect, builder or developer feel:

“This company can handle serious architectural projects and custom manufacturing.”

Use professional spacing, strong typography, high-quality architectural visuals and a sophisticated minimal design.

Do not make the website unnecessarily complicated.

Prioritize:

1. Professional appearance
2. Product presentation
3. Trust
4. Project capability
5. Easy quotation enquiries
6. Mobile responsiveness
7. Fast loading
8. SEO
9. Clear contact options
